## Modification types (real production levers)
- hook: a new opening (first 2-4 seconds): a new opening image, a new first spoken line and new on-screen text that work together. The body of the ad after the hook stays as it is, so several hooks can be edited onto the same body in one session. Read on hook_rate.
- recut: a new edit of the existing footage only: different order, pace or length (for example a 15-second version, or proof first). No new shots. Rewrite lines only to bridge the new order. Read on completion_rate or cpa.
- revoice: the same pictures with a different voice and feel: new VO delivery (who speaks, tone, pace), tightened lines, a different music bed and caption style. Read on hook_rate and ctr.
- ending: a new final scene and call to action that still sends people to the quiz. Read on ctr.
- new_angle: the same mechanism, demo and offer, told through a different concrete pain story in the setup scenes, with the payoff adjusted to match. Needs new setup shots. Read on ctr and cpa.
- new_creator: the same story beats told by a different person in their own words and setting (for example a dad, a teenager, a grandmother), as a new UGC shoot. Locked lines stay word for word. Read on cpa.

## Scope: change what the type is about, and all of it
- hook changes only the opening (scene 1, or scenes 1-2 if the opening idea needs it). Nothing after it, not even small wording fixes: a hook test must read on the opening alone.
- ending changes only the final scene.
- recut re-times and reorders; it rewrites lines only to bridge cuts.
- revoice changes delivery across the whole ad (voice, pace, music, captions); lines may be tightened everywhere.
- new_angle and new_creator are new ads: rewrite every open scene (voiceover, on-screen text and visual) for the new story or person. Only locked cells stay.
