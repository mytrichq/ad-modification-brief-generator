# Modification types

Each type is a real production lever. Two forces decide the mix:

- **Cost.** The designer already has the winner's footage. Anything cut from it ships the same day; a new shot needs a creator, a location and a shoot.
- **New reach.** Meta groups near-identical ads, so an ad that only swaps a few words is shown to the same people. To buy new reach, a variation has to look and sound like a different ad.

Cheap types give volume. Expensive types give reach. A good pack has both. Default for 10: 3 hooks, 2 new angles, 2 new creators, 1 re-cut, 1 re-voice, 1 ending.

| Type | Changes | Stays | Footage | Read the test on | Typical effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hook` | the opening (scene 1, or 1–2): image, first line, on-screen text, all together | everything after the opening | often a crop of existing footage; at most one simple new take | hook rate | light–medium |
| `recut` | order, pace, length (e.g. proof first, a 15–20s cut) | the words, except short bridges between cuts | existing only, trimmed | completion rate, CPA | medium |
| `revoice` | who speaks, tone, pace, music, caption style; lines may tighten | pictures and order | existing | hook rate, CTR | medium |
| `ending` | the final scene and the CTA (still to the quiz) | everything before it | one new shot at most | CTR | light–heavy |
| `new_angle` | the pain story in the setup and its payoff | mechanism, demo, offer (locked cells) | new setup shots, reused demo | CTR, CPA | heavy |
| `new_creator` | the person, their words, their home | the beats and the locked lines | new UGC shoot, demo reused and cropped to hands | CPA | heavy |

## Why the scope rules exist

- **A hook changes only the opening.** Hooks are tested together on the same body: if one of them also changes the ending, its result mixes two causes and the team can't tell which one moved hook rate. Fix wording problems in the original instead (the analysis step suggests fixes).
- **An ending changes only the final scene,** for the same reason.
- **New angle and new creator rewrite every open scene.** If a "new creator" variation only changes the first line, it is a hook with extra cost, and Meta will treat it as the same ad.
- **Locked cells are copied exactly.** They carry what made the ad win, and the team already checked them. Never "improve" them inside a variation.

## Choosing a mix for a specific ad

- Topic burning out (older than ~5 weeks): lean on `new_angle` and `new_creator`; the opening alone won't revive it.
- Strong body, weak start (low hook rate): more hooks, one re-cut that opens on the best proof shot.
- One narrow persona on camera: at least one `new_creator` from a different group (dad, grandparent, roommate).
- Tight shoot budget this week: hooks from existing footage, re-cut, re-voice; push new shoots to a later pack.
