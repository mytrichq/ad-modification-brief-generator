# Approved variations

Real variations of C014 "Socks for Four in Ten Seconds" (`data/scripts/C014.md`) that the reviewer approved, with their reasons. Only the changed scenes are shown; everything else is the original.

Use them as a quality bar, not as templates: a new pack for a new ad should look different.

---

## Hook · "One Pair, Tight Pull" · light to make

> Reviewer: "Reuses our demo footage as the opening. Ships today, more hooks like this."

| Scene | Footage | Visual | Voiceover | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | trim | Tight crop of the original overhead shot: one hand pulls a single sock out of the pile and its pair comes up with it, still clicked together | I pulled one sock out of the wash. Its pair came with it. | One sock out. Two come with it. |

Scenes 2–6 reused. Direction: same as original. Read on hook rate.

**Why it works**
- The opening is one image you understand with the sound off: one sock pulled, two come out.
- It is cut from footage the designer already has, so it costs an edit, not a shoot.
- Only scene 1 changes, so the hook-rate result can be trusted.

## Hook · "Count Your Single Socks" · light to make

> Reviewer: "A direct question works with the sound off and costs nothing to make."

| Scene | Footage | Visual | Voiceover | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | trim | Opening frames of the scene 4 shot, held: the overflowing basket of single socks on the dryer, slow push-in | Be honest. How many single socks are in your basket right now? | Be honest: count your single socks |

**Why it works:** it opens on a different image from the other hooks (the basket, not the pile) and turns the viewer's own laundry into the question.

## New angle · "The 7:40 Sock Hunt" · heavy

> Reviewer: "The school-bus deadline adds real urgency for parents, our biggest group."

| Scene | Footage | Visual | Voiceover | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | new | Handheld in the hallway: her kid stands in one sock holding a mismatched one while she digs through a drawer of singles, backpack by the door | Bus in five minutes, and my kid has exactly one sock. | 7:40am. One sock. Bus coming. |
| 2 | reuse | (original) | (original) | Every pair ready before the bus |
| 4 | reuse | (original) | This basket was where every school-morning sock hunt started. | No more 7:40 sock hunts |
| 6 | new | At the front door her kid pulls on a matching pair and runs out; she turns to camera and points | Take the quiz. Kid sizes, adult sizes, the whole family. | Take the quiz → |

Direction: same creator plus her school-age kid; ticking-clock percussion under scene 1 that drops out in scene 2. Read on CTR.

**Why it works**
- The pain is new and concrete (a time, a bus, a kid with one sock), so the ad reads as a different story.
- The demo and the offer (scenes 3 and 5) are reused untouched.
- Two new shots, both at home with people the creator already has.

## New creator · "Dad Does the Socks" · heavy

> Reviewer: "A real new-creator test. Book a dad from our UGC list."

| Scene | Footage | Visual | Voiceover | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | new | Selfie angle: the dad next to a heap of clean laundry on the couch pulls out sock after sock, each already in a pair | Dad on laundry duty. Socks for four of us, and I'm matching none. | Dad laundry. Zero matching. |
| 4 | trim | (original) | Our single-sock basket. On the dryer for [his real number] years. Gone. | The single-sock basket: officially retired |
| 6 | new | The dad in his kitchen holds up one clicked pair, then points at the camera | Take the quiz. Sizes for the kids, sizes for you. | Find everyone's size: take the quiz → |

Direction: a dad, 35–45, who really does his family's laundry, casual t-shirt, his own home; warm and a bit sheepish, no performance. His voice runs over the reused scenes 2–5. Read on CPA.

**Why it works**
- A different person, home and voice, so Meta treats it as a new ad and it reaches people who don't see themselves in the original.
- `[his real number]` leaves room for a true detail instead of inventing one.
- The direction is specific enough to cast from.
